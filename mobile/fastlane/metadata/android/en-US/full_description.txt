Set up a new PC and it looks like your old one in a minute.

QRPersonafy saves your desktop look to your phone: wallpaper, light or dark mode, accent color, taskbar and Start layouts, and mouse and keyboard settings. On a fresh Windows 10 or 11 PC or Linux desktop, open the QRPersonafy desktop app, scan the QR code with your phone, pick a saved PC, and it's all back. A PC saved on Windows can be restored on Linux and the other way round.

How it works
• Install the free QRPersonafy desktop app on your PC (from the project's GitHub page).
• Save a PC: choose "Save this PC" on the computer and scan the code with this app.
• Restore: choose "Restore from phone" on the new computer, scan, pick a saved PC and send it. You see what will change first, and Undo puts your old settings back.

Private by design
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no cloud run by us.
• Transfers between PC and phone are end-to-end encrypted with a one-time key from the QR code.
• Saved PCs are encrypted on your phone.
• Optional backup to your own Nextcloud or self-hosted server, encrypted on the phone before upload, so a new phone gets all your PCs back.

The phone and PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

QRPersonafy is free and open source (MIT license): https://github.com/Slackpartistic/QRPersonafy

# Privacy policy

_Applies to the QRPersonafy phone app, desktop app and backup server. Last updated 24 November 2026._

QRPersonafy does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or tracking, and no server run by the QRPersonafy project.

## What the apps handle, and where it goes

- **Your desktop settings** (wallpaper, light/dark mode, accent color, taskbar and Start layouts,
  mouse and keyboard settings) are read by the desktop app and sent to your phone over your own
  Wi-Fi, encrypted with a one-time key from the QR code. They are stored on your phone,
  encrypted, and leave it only when you send them to a PC or back them up.
- **Camera:** the phone app uses the camera only to scan the QR code on your PC's screen. Images
  are processed on the phone and never saved or sent anywhere.
- **Backups (optional):** if you turn on backup, the phone encrypts your saved PCs before
  uploading them to a server *you* choose (for example your own Nextcloud or the QRPersonafy
  backup server). That server only ever sees encrypted data. The server address, user name and
  password you enter are stored in the phone's secure storage.
- **Network:** the apps connect only to each other on your local network and, if you set it up,
  to your own backup server. They make no other internet connections.

## Deleting your data

Delete a saved PC in the phone app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Backups
live on your own server, so you can delete them there.
